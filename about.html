About Web PSEditor

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Web PSEditor is a free online image editor that runs entirely inside your web browser. Open a photo, retouch it with layers, selections, filters and text, then export the result — no software to install, no account to create, and no file ever handed over to a server.

In one sentence: a professional-grade raster editor that behaves like a desktop application, respects your privacy like a local program, and requires nothing more than a browser tab.

1. Why We Built It

Most "free online editors" work by uploading your photo to a remote server, processing it there, and sending a result back. That model is convenient, but it quietly assumes you are comfortable with a stranger's infrastructure holding your files. For an ID photo, a scanned passport, a signed contract, a medical record or an unreleased product shot, that assumption is hard to accept.

Web PSEditor was built to remove that assumption completely. Editing is performed by your own device, inside the browser tab you already have open.

2. Our Core Principle: Your Files Never Leave Your Device

The entire editing pipeline — decoding, canvas rendering, layer compositing, filter math, and file export — is executed locally with HTML5 Canvas and JavaScript. There is no upload endpoint, no processing queue, and no image storage of any kind on our side.

  • Your image is never transmitted, so it cannot be intercepted, retained or resold.
  • No sign-up, no email, no account — the editor opens and works immediately.
  • Because processing is local, the tool stays responsive even on slow connections.
  • Refreshing or closing the tab releases everything from memory.

3. What You Can Do in the Browser

Web PSEditor is built on a full layered editing engine rather than a set of one-off filters. Typical work includes:

  • Retouching and cutouts — magic wand selection, smart masking and background removal for product shots or ID photos.
  • Layered composition — multiple layers with blend modes, opacity, ordering and visibility control.
  • Color and tone — brightness, contrast, hue/saturation, curves-style adjustments and one-click filter presets.
  • Geometry — crop, rotate, straighten, flip, resize and canvas expansion.
  • Annotations and redaction — text, watermarks, mosaic and blur for hiding sensitive details.
  • Export — lossless PNG, JPEG and WEBP output with adjustable quality, or save the whole project as a multi-layer JSON file you can reopen later.

If you are new to the tool, the tutorial library walks through each workflow step by step.

4. Who Uses Web PSEditor

  • Privacy-conscious users preparing ID photos, visa photos, scans or documents that should not leave a personal device.
  • Students and teachers who need quick edits on a school computer or Chromebook without installing anything.
  • Small business owners producing product images, banners and social posts on a tight budget.
  • Designers and developers who want a fast utility for cropping, converting and redacting assets during their normal workflow.
  • Anyone on a locked-down or low-spec machine where installing a full desktop editor is impractical.

6. How the Site Stays Free

Web PSEditor has no subscription, no paywall and no watermark on exports. Hosting and bandwidth are covered by displaying advertisements through third-party networks such as Google AdSense.

Because your images are never uploaded, advertising has no technical path to your content: ads are selected from cookies and general browsing activity only. The full data-handling details are documented on our Privacy Policy page.
