How to Cut Out Image & Export Transparent PNG Online

Difficulty: Beginner • Time: 2 Mins • Format: Transparent PNG
Quick In-Browser Cutout Tool
No plugins, no subscriptions, completely private.
Launch Web PSEditor →

Creating product cards, logos, or stickers requires isolated transparent backgrounds. Follow this 3-step guide using Web PSEditor to create transparent PNG graphics easily — everything runs locally in your browser, so your images are never uploaded.

Step 1: Open Image

Drag and drop your file into Web PSEditor (or File → Open). Ensure the layer is selected in the right-hand layer dock.

Step 2: Remove the Background with the Magic Eraser

Choose the Magic Eraser Tool (magic wand icon) from the left toolbar and click once on a uniform background — every pixel of the same color disappears instantly. If the background has gradients or soft shadows, raise the Power value in the top bar to about 20–60, then click any leftover corners to clean them up. For detailed edges, use Lasso / Polygonal Select instead, then press Delete.

Step 3: Export as PNG

Click File → Save As and pick PNG (or WebP). JPG has no alpha channel — saving as JPG paints the background white again.

⚠️ Common mistakes to avoid
  • Exporting as JPG — the alpha channel is discarded and the background turns white. Always choose PNG or WebP.
  • Too low Power on gradient backgrounds — leaves ghost patches in the corners. Raise it to 30–60 and click the leftovers again.
  • Skipping the edge check — zoom to 200% and inspect hair or glass edges; a visible halo means one more erase pass with a lower Power is needed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is my exported image still white where the background should be transparent?

You most likely saved as JPG. JPG does not support transparency — re-export and choose PNG (or WebP) as the file format.

The background has a slight gradient and the first click missed parts of it. What now?

Increase the Power (tolerance) value in the top attributes bar to about 30–60, then click the remaining areas. Repeated clicks are non-destructive — press Ctrl + Z to undo at any time.

Can I keep a soft natural shadow under the product?

Yes. Lower the Power so only the flat background color is removed, or erase the background with the Lasso tool and leave the shadow area untouched.

Is my image uploaded to a server?

No. Web PSEditor runs 100% client-side — the image is processed in your browser's memory and never leaves your device.

Which formats support transparency?

PNG and WebP both keep the full alpha channel. GIF supports only 1-bit transparency, and JPG supports none.
