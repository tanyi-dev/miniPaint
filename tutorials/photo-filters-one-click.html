How to Apply

Step 1: Apply a Base Filter

Open a photo and click the Effects menu:
1. Black & White: pick a grayscale/desaturate effect — ideal for street, architecture and portraits.
2. Vintage: sepia/fade presets give an instant film vibe.
3. Cinematic: use teal-and-orange style presets with slightly raised contrast.

Step 2: Dial Back the Intensity

Default filter strength is often too strong. Lower the effect layer's opacity to 50–70% in the layers panel, or paint parts back with the eraser for a stylish "partial color" look.

Step 3: Layer Detail Effects

Add Vignette or a touch of grain/noise on top to double the film feel. Less is more.

Step 4: Export

Save via File → Save As as JPG. For social media, a long edge of 1080–1280 px balances sharpness and file size.

💡 Choosing fast: portraits love B&W, storytelling loves vintage, "movie poster" vibes love teal-and-orange.
