Method 1: Pixelate / Mosaic Effect

Select the region with the Selection Tool. Click Effects -> Mosaic / Pixelate, adjust strength, and apply.

Method 2: Smooth Gaussian Blur

Box the sensitive text, then choose Effects -> Blur -> Gaussian Blur (8-15px radius) for an aesthetic frosted-glass look.
