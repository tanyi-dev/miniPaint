Advanced: Mastering Layers & Blend Modes Online

Difficulty: Intermediate • Time: 5 Mins • Core: Non-destructive Editing
Pro-Level Multi-layer Magic
Full support for layer hierarchies, opacities, and blend modes in your browser.
Launch Web PSEditor →

The hallmark of serious image editing is non-destructive layering. Web PSEditor gives you pro-grade layer stack flexibility — every layer can carry its own opacity and blend mode, and you can change them at any time without destroying a single pixel.

Step 1: Stack Two Layers

Open your base image, then drag a second image (texture, light leak, sketch) onto the canvas — it lands as a new layer on top. The right-hand Layers panel lists both.

Step 2: Open the Composition Dialog

With the top layer selected, go to Layer -> Composition. A dialog opens with the full blend-mode dropdown and live preview on the canvas behind it.

Step 3: Pick a Mode and Confirm

Choose multiply to discard white, screen to discard black, or overlay for punchy contrast. Click OK to commit — the original layers stay untouched, so you can switch modes anytime.

⚠️ Common mistakes to avoid
  • Blending the wrong layer — the mode applies to the selected layer only. Check which layer is highlighted before opening Composition.
  • Expecting opacity to fix contrast — if Multiply looks too strong, drop the layer's Opacity (e.g. 60–80%) instead of switching modes back and forth.
  • Flattening too early — once merged, blend modes are baked in. Keep layers separate until the final export.
  • Using JPEG sources for overlays — JPG compression halos around black/white areas become visible after blending. Prefer PNG overlays.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is the blend mode option in Web PSEditor?

Select a layer, then open Layer -> Composition in the top menu. The dropdown contains all canvas blend modes — Multiply, Screen, Overlay and more.

Why did my texture disappear completely after choosing Multiply?

Multiply keeps only darker pixels. If your texture is mostly white on a light background, very little remains — try Screen for dark backgrounds, or Overlay for balanced contrast.

Can I animate or toggle the effect later?

Yes. Blend modes are non-destructive. Reopen Layer -> Composition at any time, or hide the layer with the eye icon to compare before/after.

Do blend modes work with adjustment filters?

They combine well: put a color gradient layer on Overlay, then add photo filters on the base layer — both stack non-destructively.

Is any of this uploaded to a server?

No. Layer compositing runs 100% in your browser; nothing ever leaves your device.
