Photo Color Correction Basics: Rescue Dull, Flat Photos

Difficulty: Beginner • Time: 2 min • Great for: phone snapshots, product photos
Fix dull photos instantly!
100% local color correction — originals stay on your machine.
Open Web PSEditor & Color Correct →

Straight-out-of-camera phone photos often look flat and gray — not because you shot badly, but because cameras deliberately play it safe to preserve details. A quick pass of brightness, contrast and saturation makes the image pop instantly. Many online tools upload your photos to their servers first; Web PSEditor does all the math locally in your browser, so even large images open and adjust instantly with zero privacy risk.

The Result: Before vs After

Step 1: Load Your Photo

Open Web PSEditor, click File → Open and import your photo.

Step 2: One-Click Auto Adjust (easiest)

Click Image → Auto Adjust. The editor analyzes the histogram and automatically fixes exposure and contrast. For most photos this alone solves 80% of the problem.

Step 3: Fine-Tune with Three Sliders

For a personal look, open Image → Color Corrections:
1. Brightness: lift the whole image by +5 to +15; too much blows out highlights.
2. Contrast: make the image "stand up", +10 to +25.
3. Saturation: boost colors +10 to +30; go easy on portraits to avoid orange skin.
4. Finish with Sharpen at 30–60 for crisp details.

Step 4: Export

Click File → Save As and export as JPG (quality 85+). Keep the original file so you can re-edit later.

💡 Rule of thumb: auto first, manual second; brightness before color; adjust a little at a time and keep comparing.
