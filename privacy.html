Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

Welcome to Web PSEditor. We take your privacy very seriously and are committed to safeguarding any information while you use our online image editing tool.

Core Privacy Guarantee: Web PSEditor is a 100% client-side application. All image editing, adjustments, filter processing, and exports are executed entirely inside your web browser. We do NOT upload, collect, or store your pictures on any server. Advertising and CDN partners may only see standard access metadata (such as IP address and request headers) — they never receive your image content or editing operations under any circumstances.

1. Client-Side Image Privacy

When you open an image in Web PSEditor, it is processed strictly within your local machine's memory using HTML5 Canvas technology. No image data is transmitted over the internet, ensuring complete confidentiality for your work.

2. Cookies & Local Storage

We use standard first-party cookies and browser local storage solely to remember your custom preferences, such as selected language (English/Chinese) and workspace layout.

3. Third-Party Advertising & Google AdSense

To keep this tool free for everyone, we display advertisements provided by third-party ad networks, including Google AdSense:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting AboutAds.info.
  • Advertising is served based on cookies and browsing activity only. It has zero connection to the images you open or edit in Web PSEditor — ad partners never receive, process, or profile your image data.

4. Analytics & CDN Security

We utilize Cloudflare edge network infrastructure to secure traffic and optimize global loading speeds. Cloudflare may process basic server logs (e.g. IP addresses, headers) strictly for security and anti-DDoS purposes.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact our support team at: support@webpseditor.com.
